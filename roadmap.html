          What happens to something you report

          1. You file it from the report form, which writes the issue for you.
          2. I try to reproduce it. If I cannot, I will ask you a question on the issue — you get an email, and answering it is usually what makes the fix possible.
          3. Once reproduced it is marked confirmed and given a severity: does it stop you training, is it annoying, or is it cosmetic. That is the order things get fixed in.
          4. When it ships the issue closes, it appears above, and the change is in the next Play update.

          Not on the list

          A few things are settled rather than open questions, so nobody spends a request on them. Overload will not get an account system, ads, or a server that holds your training. It is Android-only for now. And the engine will keep explaining itself — no suggestion will ever be a number with no reason attached.

          This page reads the public issue tracker from your browser when you open it, so it is never out of date. The same things, arranged as columns I actually drag cards across, are on the board. If GitHub is unreachable, or you block third-party requests, the lists here stay empty and the links still work.
