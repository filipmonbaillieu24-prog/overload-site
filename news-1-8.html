1.8
News/Version 1.8·13 October 2026

A lift can now ask for a different set each time.

The biggest update since launch, in eight short chapters. Everything below is already on your phone if you have the app. Nothing about your logged weights changed, and nothing was uploaded anywhere.

Set by set

Each set can ask for its own reps and effort.

Turn on Set by set in a lift and every set gets its own rep range and target reserve: eight to ten with two left, then one left, then nothing in the tank. REGOL judges each set against the rung it asked for.

That matters because a ramp finished exactly as written drops effort across the session by design. Against one flat target that looks like fatigue and the weight would hold. Read set by set, the same session is on target and earns the step.

Top sets, back-offs, anything that gets harder as it goes: this is the release that understands it.
Why these weights

Every lift explains itself before you start.

Today’s card opens the reasoning for every lift in the session at once, in the same words the live screen uses when you get there.

The label beside each weight says what was decided. A weight that holds says holds. Red is kept for a weight that actually comes down.

Make it yours

True black, and a colour of your own.

A third theme for OLED screens and night sessions. Not Dark with the page turned black: the whole stack of surfaces was re-laid so cards still read as layers.

Pick a hue and brightness of your own. There is a floor per hue so the black ink on top stays readable: the worst case across every colour is exactly 7:1, and a test holds it there. A program can carry its own colour, worn only while you train it.

Every colour you can choose is held to at least 7:1 against the ink on top of it.
Moments

A new best gets a moment.

Beat a record and the screen takes over in your accent, with the number at full size, a rising triple buzz, the bell struck twice when rest ends, and one flash of motion. It is the only celebration in the app and nothing else uses it.

Full, Quiet or Off in settings, and the system’s reduce-motion setting is honoured whatever you choose. One line at the end of a workout reads the session for you. Observational, never praise.

What you can log now

Timed sets, bodyweight, and a records board.

Planks, carries and hangs are logged in seconds instead of pretending to be forty-five reps. The engine does not progress them and says so on the card, because every rule it has is written in reps.

Bodyweight is a dated series, so a session counts what you weighed then, and assisted and weighted pull-ups finally count the body in your totals. The records board shows your heaviest set for 1, 3, 5, 8 and 10 reps side by side.

Sharing

Templates and programs travel as a code. Cards for every record.

Hand a friend a template or a whole program as a code their camera reads, or as a file. Everything is inside the code, because there is no server to fetch it from, which is why it works with the network off.

New share cards for a record and for a lift’s chart, and a footer on every card you choose: your streak, how long you have trained, a bodyweight ratio, or a gym name. Show them in kilograms, pounds, or exactly as you logged it.

The whole program is inside the code. Nothing is uploaded to make it.
Also in 1.8

Smaller things you will notice in a session.

Reps in reserve week by week in a block, so an easy week in the middle that is not a deload is finally possible. Sort your lifts by recent, progress or name. A streak warning two days before a run of three weeks or more would be lost.

Weighted pull-ups keep their added weight, a review is asked for once and never after a deload or a record, and the engine is untouched where it matters: every weight it proposes and every sentence it says are the same as in 1.7.

Same engine, same file on your phone. 352 tests pass, up from 231.
Out now

1.8 is on Google Play.

Free for everyone until 2027. No account, no ads. Your data file is the same one, so there is nothing to migrate.
