A training log that tells you what to lift next

Overload watches your reps and your reps in reserve, and proposes the next weight with its reasoning attached. It runs on your phone, with no account and no server.

Early access

Overload is in early access. It is a real training app doing real work — the progression engine, the logging and the stats are what I use myself — but it is young, it will change, and you may meet a rough edge. Tell me and it gets fixed.

What it does

  • Proposes each lift's weight from your logged reps and RIR, and says why.
  • Templates, programs and mesocycle blocks, with the week following your training rather than the calendar.
  • A rest timer that reacts to how the set went, on the lock screen and in a notification.
  • Charts, records and a weekly breakdown of hard sets per muscle.
  • Guides for 1,577 exercises, bundled in the app — no network needed.
  • Export, import and automatic backups to a folder you choose.

What it costs

Free for the first month, then €1 a month. Cancel any time in Google Play. Without a subscription Overload keeps working: you can train, log every set and use the progression engine, with up to 3 templates and 1 program, and without the charts and notes. Nothing you make is ever deleted.

Every subscription goes back into building the app — new features, fixes, no ads, no accounts.

Your data

Everything stays in one file on your phone. There is no account to make and no server to send it to. See the privacy policy.
