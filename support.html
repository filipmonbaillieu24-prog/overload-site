Support

Write to me and you get me.

REGOL is made by one person. What helps: what you did, what happened, and what you expected. A screenshot is worth a paragraph. If it involves your training data, You → Data → Export file and attach the JSON: it is only your own workouts, and it lets me reproduce the problem exactly.

Your data

Backups, new phones, other apps.

REGOL stores everything on your phone, so a backup is up to you. The privacy policy covers what it stores, and where.

Keeping your data safe

One-off copy: You → Data → Export file, then send it somewhere safe. Automatic: You → Data → Backups, pick a folder (a cloud-synced one is ideal). REGOL writes a backup after each workout and keeps the last ten. Restoring: You → Data → Import file → Replace.

Moving to a new phone

Export on the old phone, install REGOL on the new one, then Import → Replace. If you have Google’s device backup on, Android restores the file for you when you set up the new phone.

Coming from another app

Strong and Hevy CSV exports import directly, and REGOL matches them to your lifts. You → Data → Import file, then pick the CSV. Sets imported without an effort rating progress by reps until you log your own.

Questions

Asked first.

The subscription questions do not apply yet: REGOL is free for everyone through 31 December 2026. See what it costs after.

Why did the weight it suggested stay the same?

The engine reads your logged reps and how many you had left. Exactly on target holds the weight and asks for one more rep. Under target holds rather than drops. Each card says which it was, and you can always overrule it.

Why did it offer a deload?

Two sessions in a row under the effort you were aiming for. It is an offer, about ten percent lighter, and you can decline it. A lift that has not set a new best in four sessions also gets the offer.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-in and no server.

Does it work offline?

Yes, completely. The only thing that needs a connection is checking your subscription with Google Play, and the last answer is remembered.

How do I cancel?

In the Play Store: your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → REGOL → Cancel. You keep everything until the period you paid for runs out, then the app falls back to the free plan. Nothing you made is deleted.

I subscribed but the app still looks locked

Open You → I already subscribed. That asks Play again. If it still says free plan, check you are signed in to the Play account that subscribed.

I paid but I want a refund

Google handles the money, so refunds go through Google Play. If something about the app pushed you to it, tell me too.

Can I delete everything?

You → Delete everything wipes the app. To remove every trace at once, uninstall it. If you turned on Health Connect, remove what REGOL wrote there first, from the same settings screen.
