Bugs and ideas

Tell me what’s wrong.

Fill in the form on the next page and press the button. It opens a ready-made issue on GitHub with everything already written; you just press Submit there. No GitHub account? The second button sends the same thing by email. Either way it lands on the public roadmap, so you can watch it go from reported to shipped.

Why it works like this: the site is plain files with no server behind it, for the same reason the app has no account. The form writes your issue and hands it to GitHub, where your own account posts it, so you get notified when it is fixed.

The form

Something is broken.

What you did, what happened, what you expected. A screenshot is worth a paragraph.

Issues are public, so don’t paste anything you’d rather keep private. Screenshots can be dragged onto the issue once it opens.

The situation in the gym is more useful than the feature. It usually leads somewhere better.
